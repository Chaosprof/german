# Independent validation: high-severity verb findings

Scope: all 190 records in `validation-batches/verb_high.json`, checked against the current JSON data and independently adjudicated for meaning, valency/reflexivity, separability, principal parts, and Perfekt auxiliary. `CONFIRM` means the reported defect and high severity stand; `REVISE` means a high-severity defect remains but the proposed linguistic analysis or repair needed correction; `DOWNGRADE` means a real but lower-severity issue remains; `REJECT` means the reported defect does not stand.

1. **verbs_001 — schaffen — CONFIRM.** The weak *schaffte/geschafft* belongs to “manage/accomplish,” while “create” normally requires strong *schuf/geschaffen*; the mixed gloss must be split or sense-labelled.
2. **verbs_001 — ziehen — CONFIRM.** Transitive “pull” takes *haben*; intransitive relocation (*nach Berlin ziehen*) takes *sein*.
3. **verbs_001 — treten — CONFIRM.** Directional stepping takes *sein*, whereas kicking/treading on something takes *haben*.
4. **verbs_001 — stoßen — CONFIRM.** Transitive pushing takes *haben*; directional collision (*gegen etwas stoßen*) takes *sein*.
5. **verbs_001 — hängen — CONFIRM.** Strong *hing/gehangen* is the intransitive/state verb; transitive “hang something” is weak *hängte/gehängt*.
6. **verbs_001 — einziehen — CONFIRM.** Moving in is *ist eingezogen*; collecting taxes/debts is *hat eingezogen*.
7. **verbs_002 — durchlaufen — CONFIRM.** Separable physical *lief durch/ist durchgelaufen* must not cover inseparable transitive *durchlief/hat durchlaufen* “undergo/go through.”
8. **verbs_002 — stürzen — CONFIRM.** Intransitive falling takes *sein*; transitive overthrow/topple takes *haben*.
