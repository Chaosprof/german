## Errors

- **wenden** | `praeteritum`/`perfekt` | severity=high | category=verbform
  current: irregular, `wandte` / `gewandt`, gloss "turn/apply to"
  correct: for the first glossed sense (turn a car, turn a page, turn food) the verb is weak: `wendete` / `gewendet`; `wandte/gewandt` belongs only to *sich an jdn wenden* / *anwenden*
  why: two verbs in one entry — the everyday concrete sense is given the wrong (strong) conjugation.

- **auftauchen** | `auxiliary` | severity=high | category=aux
  current: `UNSET` (app renders "hat"), gloss "appear/emerge"
  correct: `sein` — *er ist plötzlich aufgetaucht*
  why: change of state / appearance verb; "hat aufgetaucht" is wrong.

- **begeben** | `auxiliary` | severity=high | category=aux
  current: `sein`, gloss "go/proceed"
  correct: `haben` — *er hat sich auf den Weg begeben*; gloss should read "sich begeben — go/proceed/make one's way"
  why: reflexive-only verb, and reflexives always take *haben*.

- **angehen** | `auxiliary` | severity=medium | category=aux
  current: `sein`, gloss "concern/tackle"
  correct: `haben` — *das hat mich nicht angegangen*, *er hat das Problem angegangen*
  why: *sein* applies only to the unglossed intransitive sense "start up / come on (light, fire)"; both glossed transitive senses take *haben*.

- **hängen** | `praeteritum`/`perfekt` | severity=medium | category=verbform
  current: irregular `hing` / `gehangen`, gloss just "hang"
  correct: split senses — intransitive "be hanging" = `hing/gehangen`; transitive "hang (sth) up" = weak `hängte/gehängt`
  why: with a bare gloss the learner will produce "ich habe das Bild gehangen", which is wrong.

- **senden** | `praeteritum`/`perfekt` | severity=medium | category=verbform
  current: irregular `sandte` / `gesandt`, gloss "send/broadcast"
  correct: the "broadcast" sense is weak only (`sendete/gesendet`); for "send" the weak forms are also the commoner modern ones
  why: strong forms are wrong for one of the two glossed senses.

- **schaffen** | `praeteritum`/`perfekt` | severity=medium | category=verbform

  correct: the A1 sense is "manage/get done" = weak `schaffte/geschafft`; that should be the form pair carried in the fields
  why: the drilled forms belong to the rarer literary sense "create", so the app teaches the wrong forms for the learner sense.
