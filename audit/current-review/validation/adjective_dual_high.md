# Independent validation: adjective and dual-gender high findings

Scope: all 235 findings in `adjective_high.json`, followed by all 9 findings in `dual_gender_high.json` (244 total). I evaluated each finding against the current row and did not treat the first-pass recommendation as presumptively correct.

Method note: a productive participle is not automatically an invalid adjective-bank member. IDS/grammis explicitly treats Partizip I as an adjective derivation and Partizip II as a verb form that can also be used adjectivally; attributive participles decline like adjectives ([IDS adjective inflection](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/kontrastive-grammatik/3609), [IDS participles](https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/sgt/2240?termini=term)). Therefore, a correct participle with a correct gloss is generally a **medium deck-scope/lemma-policy issue**, not a high-severity false fact. Rare lexical facts were also checked against Duden where needed (for example [internal](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/internal), [restaurativ](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/restaurativ), [clean](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/clean), [pflanzlich](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/pflanzlich), [kratzfest](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/kratzfest), and [Kurkuma](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Kurkuma)).
