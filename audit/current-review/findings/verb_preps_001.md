- **anpassen an** | `verb / english / example` | severity=medium | category=verbform
  current: `anpassen an + Akk` — `adapt to` — `Er passt sich an die Umgebung an.`
  correct: Store `sich anpassen an + Akk` for this gloss and example, or use a transitive model such as `Er passt den Plan an die Lage an.`
  why: Non-reflexive `etwas an etwas anpassen` is valid, but the intransitive sense shown here requires `sich`; the headword and example currently teach different valency frames.
